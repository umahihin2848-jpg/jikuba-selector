# Ticket EV backtest — coherent PL probability × historical odds (2026-10-17)

## Goal
Test whether the coherent Plackett-Luce probability layer can be used directly as a betting-value probability for win / quinella / wide by buying tickets whose `model probability × odds >= 1.20`.

This is a validation report only. No production buy rule is deployed from this result.

## Data and protocol
- Frozen Ability v1 raw logit is the horse-strength input.
- One race-order distribution is generated with the previously selected PL temperature `tau=0.955462`.
- Win / quinella / wide probabilities are derived from the same race-order distribution.
- Historical results: TARGET result exports.
- Historical odds: `2016_2025_odds.csv` and `2026_odds.csv`.
- Historical settlement: HR payout records in `haitou.txt` / `2026_haitou.txt`.
- Wide purchase EV uses the displayed **lower wide odds**; settlement uses the actual HR wide payout.
- 2025 universe reconstructed with the pilot protocol: 401 races / 6,060 runners.
- 2026 universe reconstructed with the pilot protocol: 297 races / 4,526 runners.
- These are reconstruction universes, not exact canonical training matrices.

Important execution caveat: the provided odds export behaves like final/closing odds (e.g. winning quinella odds match the HR settlement price; wide settlement lies inside the exported wide range). Therefore this is a **closing-price feasibility backtest**, not a T-15 executable backtest.

## Sanity check of odds parsing
Example: 2026 毎日王冠.
- Winning quinella 1-13: exported odds `124.8`, HR payout `124.8x`.
- Winning wide 1-13: exported range `26.9–28.6`, HR payout `28.2x`.

The wide matrix begins at the fixed export column used by TARGET; an earlier dynamic-offset interpretation was rejected after this settlement cross-check.

## Test 1 — naive rule: buy every ticket with EV >= 1.20
This fails badly in both years.

### 2025
| Ticket | Bets | ROI |
|---|---:|---:|
| Win | 3,209 | 83.6% |
| Wide | 21,828 | 55.8% |
| Quinella | 26,249 | 58.4% |

With example fixed stakes (win 1,500 / wide 1,000 / quinella 500), combined ROI is approximately **60.1%**.

### 2026
| Ticket | Bets | ROI |
|---|---:|---:|
| Win | 2,306 | 59.8% |
| Wide | 15,916 | 67.4% |
| Quinella | 19,167 | 64.1% |

With the same example stakes, combined ROI is approximately **65.4%**.

### Interpretation
The probability layer can be reasonably calibrated in broad probability bands while still producing false value signals in the extreme longshot tail. `p × odds` magnifies small probability-model errors when odds are very large. Therefore calibration alone is insufficient for a betting edge.

## Test 2 — require a minimum model probability as well as EV >= 1.20
Exploratory probability floors were added to prevent very-low-probability longshots from dominating the candidate set:
- Win: `p >= 10%`
- Wide: `p >= 8%`
- Quinella: `p >= 2%`

These are diagnostic floors, not approved production thresholds.

### 2025
| Ticket | Bets | ROI |
|---|---:|---:|
| Win | 383 | 53.2% |
| Wide | 748 | 78.0% |
| Quinella | 1,994 | 100.7% |

Combined example-stake portfolio:
- 3,125 tickets / 381 races
- Invested 2,319,500 yen
- Returned 1,893,350 yen
- ROI **81.6%**
- Profit **-426,150 yen**

### 2026
| Ticket | Bets | ROI |
|---|---:|---:|
| Win | 247 | 96.5% |
| Wide | 530 | 111.6% |
| Quinella | 1,448 | 136.8% |

Combined example-stake portfolio:
- 2,225 tickets / 276 races
- Invested 1,624,500 yen
- Returned 1,939,500 yen
- ROI **119.4%**
- Profit **+315,000 yen**

The year-to-year sign reversal means this is **not a stable replicated edge**.

## Single-win-EV anchor diagnostic
Restricting wide/quinella tickets to pairs containing at least one horse with win EV >=1.20 does not create a stable improvement.

Using the same probability floors:
- 2025 combined anchored ROI: **79.0%**
- 2026 combined anchored ROI: **123.1%**

Again the direction flips across years.

## Variance / uncertainty
Race-bootstrap 95% ROI intervals for the diagnostic floor rules are wide and all include 1.00:

### 2025
- Win p>=10%, EV>=1.20: ROI 53.2%, CI about 27.0–82.7%
- Wide p>=8%, EV>=1.20: ROI 78.0%, CI about 48.1–114.0%
- Quinella p>=2%, EV>=1.20: ROI 100.7%, CI about 55.6–154.8%

### 2026
- Win p>=10%, EV>=1.20: ROI 96.5%, CI about 57.5–139.3%
- Wide p>=8%, EV>=1.20: ROI 111.6%, CI about 48.4–206.2%
- Quinella p>=2%, EV>=1.20: ROI 136.8%, CI about 52.5–256.9%

Quinella is particularly payout-concentrated. In 2026, the largest winning quinella contributes roughly one-third of total quinella returns in this rule, and the top three winners contribute more than half.

## Decision
### Rejected
- Do **not** deploy `raw PL probability × odds >= 1.20` as a buy rule.
- Do **not** interpret broad probability calibration as proof of positive expected betting value.
- Do **not** set production probability floors from these exploratory 2025/2026 results.

### Keep
- The coherent race-order probability architecture remains useful as a structural probability layer.
- Win / quinella / wide can still be derived consistently from one ranking distribution.

### Next statistical step
The value model must be validated **conditional on the market**, not only marginally calibrated. The next model should test whether Ability/M3/structure adds predictive information beyond market-implied probability, with a rule frozen on an earlier development sample and evaluated on a later untouched sample.

For deployable betting validation, T-15 odds (or another actually available purchase-time snapshot) are required. Closing odds can be used as a diagnostic benchmark but must not be labelled an executable live ROI backtest.
